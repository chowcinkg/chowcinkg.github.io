About

I earned my Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Sumandeep Vidyapeeth in India and have past experience as a dentist and dental assistant in India and Canada. My experience spans general dentistry, prevention, infection control, and multidisciplinary clinical workflows.

Some of my most formative work happened aboard a mobile dental van in rural Gujarat, where I screened patients, taught preventive care, and coordinated referrals in resource-limited settings. That experience shaped my belief that clear, culturally sensitive communication is as essential to good dentistry as clinical skill.

I most recently completed a Postgraduate Diploma in Pharmaceutical Regulatory Affairs and Quality Control at Seneca Polytechnic, earning a place on the President's Honour List. I am an aspiring international dentist seeking admission to an advanced standing dental program in the United States.
